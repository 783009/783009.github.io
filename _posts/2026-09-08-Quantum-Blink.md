# Quantum Blink

Step into the shoes of **Bill Nylon the Science Guy** and attempt to escape 10 dangerous rooms in an infected, high-tech laboratory! The lab has been completely overrun by swarms of chaotic, hyper-aggressive mutants—including projectile-spitting pests that feel tormentingly familiar and armored creepy-crawlies nesting in every corner. 

To unlock the door to the next room, you have to clear out every single enemy. Your only weapon? A short-range teleportation ability. You'll need to precisely time your blinks to teleport directly *through* enemies to defeat them, while also using the same mechanic to navigate tricky platforming hazards and traverse the map. 

The game was built using Processing, converting the original `.pde` files over to JavaScript to make it completely playable right in your web browser.
